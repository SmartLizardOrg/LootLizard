01 / THE GAME PLAN

One browser tab.
One less thing to think about.

Keep LootLizard on your other monitor.
Everything starts with the map you already use.

  1. 01

    Share your game screen.

    Choose your game screen in the browser’s sharing dialog. Give permission once per session; stop sharing whenever you want.

  2. 02

    Open the map. Play on.

    LootLizard checks for your map every second by default. Adjust the interval in Settings, from half a second to ten seconds.

  3. 03

    Pick the next destination.

    Your last map stays visible while you play. Pick a marked location, then open the map in game whenever you want to refresh it.

02 / YOUR SCREEN STAYS YOURS

The heavy lifting
happens right here.

Image processing runs on your computer, inside your browser. Your screenshots aren’t sent to a server for recognition.

Map images and location data come with the site. Your game captures stay on your device, and you control when sharing stops.
